### Major revamps & reworks
- **Kaja** (Aug 2026, patch 2.1.95) — "Nazar King" rework: S2 lets him **fly over walls**, ult is now an **area suppression** that drags multiple enemies
- **Hanzo** — risk model flipped: your body is **invincible while ulting**, but Hanekage dying **doubles your ult cooldown**
- **Kimmy** — reworked with real CC (immobilizing beam, group-pulling ult, wall-vaulting jetpack)
- **Eudora** (Jan 2026) — Superconductor kit: same single-target burst + new AoE combo damage
- **Aulus** (Apr 2026) — new basic-attack-weaving kit with dash + new ult
- **Alice, Gloo, Melissa** — all reworked/adjusted and now meta-relevant

### System & item changes
- **Season 41 jungle rework (2.1.88):** Retribution damage raised, creep pacing reshaped. Tank junglers got overtuned → **Baxia, Fredrinn, Akai trimmed in 2.1.90**
- **Economy anti-snowball:** less gold from minions/creeps, more passive late income — comebacks are more live than before
- **Item shakeups:** Dominance Ice + Black Ice Shield reworked into an enemy-proximity-scaling defense line · **Twilight Armor is gone, replaced by Chastise Pauldron** (reduces attacker's attack speed) · Guardian Helmet gained an excess-damage reduction passive
- **Roam/support survivability buffs (2.1.88):** tanks and backline supports got durability — this is why the support/tank class is dominating ranked right now

### The current meta in one paragraph
Utility and sustain supports are running the game from the roam slot (Marcel, Rafaela, Floryn, Estes all 53%+ WR). The jungle belongs to lockdown/utility assassins and the reworked Hanzo rather than pure burst. EXP is about split-push pressure and unkillable bruisers (Sun, Masha, Gloo). Mid favors zone/control mages (Zetian, Zhuxin, Yve, Kadita, Gord). Gold lane is an on-hit marksman meta (Melissa, Hanabi) with the revamped Kimmy as a magic-damage flex pick.

## 🗡️ JUNGLE

### 1. Hirara — "Fallen Scarlet" ⭐ NEW HERO
**S+ pick priority · 52.1% WR · 52.2% ban rate** — the most banned hero in the game.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Tough Boots → Hunter Strike → Blade of Despair → Blade of the Heptaseas → Malefic Roar → Immortality |
| **Emblem** | Assassin — Rupture / Seasoned Hunter / Killing Spree |
| **Spell** | Retribution |

*Cross-checked: the Hunter Strike + BoD + Heptaseas core is confirmed by independent 2026 guides; Malefic Roar (vs tanks), Rose Gold Meteor (vs burst), and Immortality (late game) are the flex slots.*

**How she works:** She has **no ultimate** — two skills that *merge* into two super-casts. Drag S1 into S2 (**Infernal Torrent**) = spinning engage that pins everyone caught. Drag S2 into S1 (**Falling Maple**) = untargetable burst + escape dash. Two energy charges decide whether you can engage, escape, or both.
**Quick combo:** S1 in → S1+S2 merge (immobilize) → S2+S1 merge (burst + dash out).
**Draft tip:** She's a lockdown duelist, not a brawler. Avoid into Saber, Phoveus, Valentina, Lunox, or point-click CC (Atlas, Franco, Khufra).

### 2. Hanzo — "Akuma Ninja" 🔧 REWORKED
**S tier · 55.8% WR · 24% ban rate** — one of the biggest risers of Season 41.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Swift Boots → Corrosion Scythe → Demon Hunter Sword → Golden Staff → Malefic Roar → Hunter Strike |
| **Emblem** | Assassin |
| **Spell** | Retribution |

**How he works:** The rework flipped his risk model — **your real body is invincible while the ult is active**; the fragile part is the Hanekage ghost you pilot (and its Energy). Kills the enemy carry through walls and walks out untouched. Every Hanekage death **doubles your ult cooldown**, so don't feed the ghost.
**Quick combo:** Ult from safety → S2 dash-in (knock airborne) → Black Mist + basics to stack passive → S1 for max damage.
**Draft tip:** Hunts immobile carries (Lesley, Layla, Pharsa, Cyclops). Avoid into Fanny/Helcurt mirrors and lockdown tanks.

## ⚔️ EXP LANE

### 5. Masha — "Wild-oats Fist" (highest WR EXP laner)
**S tier · 57% WR**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Warrior Boots → War Axe → Corrosion Scythe → Thunder Belt → Queen's Wings → Immortality |
| **Emblem** | Fighter |
| **Spell** | Sprint |

**How she works:** Three HP bars, and **losing a bar is the payoff** — it triggers a damage-ignore + cleanse, refreshes Thunderclap, and makes skills cheaper. Basics scale with the *enemy's* max HP, so she's the answer to HP-stacking tanks (Hylos, Gloo, Belerick, Khufra).
**Quick combo:** S1 to close → S2 slow → basics.
**Draft tip:** Avoid into suppression (Franco, Kaja, Barats) and long-range poke (Pharsa, Yve, Zhuxin).

### 8. Gloo — "Swamp Spirits" 🔧 REVAMPED (2nd most banned hero)
**S tier · 54.5% WR · 48.5% ban rate** — flexes EXP or roam.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Tough Boots → Cursed Helmet → Glowing Wand → Oracle → Antique Cuirass → Immortality |
| **Emblem** | Basic Common (hybrid regen page; Tank emblem also standard) |
| **Spell** | Flicker |

**How he works:** The goo-reset loop: stack Sticky on a carry, **attach with the ult**, and turn them into a piece of your own equipment — you redirect their position while your team punishes. Two nerf rounds (2.1.61, 2.1.88) trimmed his numbers but the loop is intact.
**Quick combo:** S1+S2 to close and immobilize → S1 + ult stacks → attach to max-stack target → drag host out of position.
**Draft tip:** Pick into stationary ranged carries (Cecilion, Yve, Wanwan). Avoid into Faramis, Moskov, Alice.

## 🔮 MID LANE

### 9. Zetian — "Celestial Empress"
**A tier · 50.9% WR · 9.3% ban rate**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Magic Boots → Glowing Wand → Fleeting Time → Wishing Lantern → Divine Glaive → Blood Wings |
| **Emblem** | Mage — Rupture / Bargain Hunter / Impure Rage |
| **Spell** | Flicker |

*Community variant: many Zetian mains run **Enchanted Talisman** (instead of Glowing Wand) for mana + more ult spam, and Fleeting Time is popular because assist-uptime refunds her long ult CD. Same core direction either way: CDR + utility + Wishing Lantern/Divine Glaive.*

**How she works:** Global-reach control mage. Ult = team-wide movement speed buff + global stun volley. Best when enemies cluster or the enemy jungler rotates without consequence.
**Quick combo (this order matters):** **S2 first** (places spirit, shreds magic defense, slows) → S1 through the zone for amplified damage → ult when they're grouped.
**Draft tip:** Needs a named frontliner between her and their jungler. Avoid vs 2+ mobile divers (Saber, Ling, Fanny, Nolan).

## 🏹 GOLD LANE

### 12. Melissa — "Cursed Needle" 🔧 REWORKED (best gold laner in ranked)
**S tier · 56.3% WR · 10.3% ban rate**

Two legitimate builds right now — pick based on enemy comp:

| | **Build A — Trinity On-Hit (community standard)** |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Swift Boots → Corrosion Scythe → Demon Hunter Sword → Golden Staff → Wind of Nature → Malefic Roar |
| **Emblem** | Marksman — Swift / Weapon Master / Weakness Finder (or Quantum Charge) |
| **Spell** | Flicker (or Inspire) |

| | **Build B — Crit Hybrid (win-rate optimized post-rework)** |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Swift Boots → Haas's Claws → Berserker's Fury → Demon Hunter Sword → Malefic Gun → Windtalker |
| **Emblem** | Marksman — Fatal / Weapon Master / Weakness Finder |
| **Spell** | Inspire |

**How she works:** Zone-tether DPS. The 2.1.67 rework removed the S1 cooldown loop — blink now chains straight into Muddles, and the ult gives an on-cast shield instead of passive defense. Go Away's knockback perimeter still ruins divers (Lancelot, Fanny, Hayabusa burn engage tools on the barrier).
**Which build?** Trinity shreds tanks and sustain comps (DHS + Golden Staff multiply the Muddles link damage). The crit hybrid spikes harder vs squishy lineups and is what the highest-win-rate Melissa games currently run. Vs dive-heavy teams, both should sneak in Wind of Nature or Rose Gold Meteor.
**Quick combo:** S2 link → S1 to extend → ult to keep them off you → basics.
**Draft tip:** Best with lockdown engage on her team. Avoid vs Franco (hook ignores the barrier), Lolita (blocks S2), and long-range poke (Lesley, Xavier, Granger).

## 🛡️ ROAM (TANK & SUPPORT)

### 14. Marcel — "Split-Soul Artist" ⭐ NEW HERO (highest WR in the game)
**S tier · 58.8% WR · 32.2% ban rate**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Build** | Tough Boots → Thunder Belt → Dominance Ice → Oracle → Athena's Shield → Immortality |
| **Emblem** | Support — Agility / Pull Yourself Together / Focusing Mark |
| **Spell** | Petrify (Vengeance vs basic-attack-heavy teams) |

*Cross-checked: four independent guides agree on the Thunder Belt → Dominance Ice → Oracle core. His damage scales with hybrid defense, so tank items ARE his damage items. Antique Cuirass or Chastise Pauldron are standard flex swaps for the 5th slot.*

**How he works:** Turns one chokepoint into a kill box. Patch 2.1.88 traded his old perma-lockdown for a heavy slow (S2) + a **freeze window the whole team can now shoot into** — he hands your carry a pocket of free damage.
**Quick combo:** S2 → S1 → run into their formation → S2 again to dash out → S1 on top of them → ult on the immobilized pile.
**Draft tip:** Pick into stand-and-shoot comps (Cecilion, Vexana, Hanabi, Xavier). Avoid into mobility that walks out of slows (Ling, Harith) and don't pair him with scatter-dive comps.

## 🎖️ Emblem Cheat Sheet (current talent meta)

**Spell defaults:** Retribution (jungle) · Flicker (most lanes) · Inspire (basic-attack marksmen) · Petrify (Marcel/engage supports) · Aegis (Hanabi) · Sprint (Masha/Kimmy).

## 🧠 Situational Item Swaps (the part that actually wins games)

Never build the same six items every game. Swap the last 1–2 slots based on what's killing you:

| Threat | Buy |
|--------|-----|
| Skill-based physical damage (fighters/assassins) | Dreadnought Armor, Antique Cuirass |
| Magic burst (Eudora, Aurora, Vale) | Athena's Shield, Rose Gold Meteor |
| Sustained magic damage (Valir, Chang'e) | Radiant Armor, Rose Gold Meteor |
| Physical burst (Lesley, Granger) | Winter Crown, Wind of Nature, Blade Armor |
| HP-stacking tanks | Demon Hunter Sword (phys), Glowing Wand + Divine Glaive (magic) |
| Getting dove every fight | Winter Crown, Immortality, Wind of Nature |

**Classic synergies to remember:** Holy Crystal + Blood Wings (shield scales with magic power) · Haas's Claws + Berserker's Fury (crit lifesteal core) · War Axe + Endless Battle (fighter tempo core) · Oracle + any regen/shield kit.
