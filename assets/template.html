Major revamps & reworks

Kaja (Aug 2026) — "Nazar King": S2 flies over walls, ult is now an area suppression that drags multiple enemies.

Hanzo — risk model flipped: real body is invincible while ulting; Hanekage dying doubles ult cooldown.

Kimmy — real CC added: immobilizing beam, group-pulling ult, wall-vaulting jetpack. All damage is magic.

Eudora (Jan 2026) — Superconductor kit: single-target burst + new AoE combo damage.

Aulus (Apr 2026), Alice, Gloo, Melissa — all reworked into meta relevance.

Hirara — was ~45–46% WR at release (players found her too hard); now the most banned hero after the playerbase learned her + 2.1.90's jungle economy shifted toward carry junglers.

System & item changes

Season 41 jungle rework (2.1.88): stronger Retribution, reshaped creep pacing. Tank junglers (Akai, Baxia, Fredrinn) got overtuned → trimmed in 2.1.90, which also raised early–mid jungle gold and big-creep rewards by ~20%.

Anti-snowball economy: less gold from minions/creeps, more passive late income — comebacks are more live than you remember.

Items: Dominance Ice + Black Ice Shield reworked into proximity-scaling defense · Twilight Armor replaced by Chastise Pauldron (cuts attacker's attack speed) · Guardian Helmet gained excess-damage reduction.

2.1.88 roam buffs: tanks and backline supports got survivability — why supports/tanks now dominate ranked.

The meta in one paragraph

Utility and sustain supports are running the game from roam (Marcel, Rafaela, Floryn, Estes all 53%+ WR). The jungle belongs to lockdown/utility assassins and the reworked Hanzo rather than pure burst. EXP is about split-push pressure and unkillable bruisers (Sun, Masha, Gloo). Mid favors zone/control mages (Zetian, Yve, Kadita, Gord). Gold lane is an on-hit marksman meta (Melissa, Hanabi) with magic-damage Kimmy as the flex pick.

Editorial cross-checks we kept: Hayabusa is S+ in esports lists but 47.6% WR in ranked — great in coordinated hands, coin-flip in solo queue. Zhuxin is S in editorial lists but 47.7% WR in ranked — she needs a real front line.
